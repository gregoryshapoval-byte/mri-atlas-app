## Content and sources

- SNO/EANO 2025, Wen et al., DOI 10.1093/neuonc/noaf177, Neuroimaging and Surgical Planning: heterogeneous enhancing necrotic mass, connected FLAIR abnormalities, high perfusion/cellularity, nonenhancing exceptions, inability to reliably separate oedema from infiltrating nonenhancing tumour.
- WHO CNS5 summary 2021, DOI 10.1093/neuonc/noab106: glioblastoma IDH-wildtype CNS WHO grade 4 distinct from astrocytoma IDH-mutant grade 4. Module does not infer molecular status or assign definitive grade from MRI.
- SNO/EANO 2020, DOI 10.1093/neuonc/noaa106, Diagnosis and Imaging: ADC/cellularity, rCBV, haemorrhage and MRS limits. No diagnostic numeric cut-off or probability calculator introduced.
- BTIP 2015, DOI 10.1093/neuonc/nov095; Table 1 and footnotes, text: matched isotropic T1 before/after; goal 1mm, acceptable <=1.5mm; 2D T2/FLAIR/DWI <=4mm no gap, 5mm allowance for older 1.5T; b0/500/1000, three directions and older-system b0/1000 allowance; T2 between injection and post T1, 4–8min window; standard 0.1mmol/kg distinguished from agent-specific current dosing. Additional SWI/DSC/MRS/DTI/fMRI separated from minimum protocol.
- EHA–ESMO PCNSL 2024, DOI 10.1002/hem3.89: deep/periventricular, homogeneous enhancement, ADC restriction; steroid issue relayed to clinical team rather than instructing patient medication changes.
- Bauer et al. 2015, PMID 25845813: peritumoral perfusion for solitary metastasis differential. Hartmann et al. 2001, PMID 11673170: restricted centre not pathognomonic for abscess.
- Lucchinetti et al. 2008, PMID 18535080 / DOI 10.1093/brain/awn098: variability of tumefactive demyelination, open ring not required or conclusive.
